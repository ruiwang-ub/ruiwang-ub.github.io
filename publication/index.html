AI Search and Information Exposure

When an AI search engine writes the answer, which news sources and perspectives does it bring into view? I audit how the wording of a question and a user’s disclosed political identity relate to the sources AI systems cite. I’m also developing experiments to test how readers evaluate answers when the argument and the cited sources agree with or challenge their views.

  • Conference research on AI search citations

    APSA Annual Meeting, Boston · 2026 · Conference paper

    An audit of how AI search engines cite news sources across ideologically framed questions.

AI, Journalism, and Trust

I study how people judge AI-assisted news and fact-checks. My experiments examine how an AI or human authorship label, information about how the work was produced, and expectations about machine objectivity shape perceptions of credibility, bias, and trust.

Digital Platforms, Identity, and Public Discourse

I examine how political identities and platform features shape what gains attention and how people take part in public debate. My collaborators and I have traced how social justice discourse became politicized across platforms, how partisan arguments entered news-app reviews, and how scientific controversies moved across news and social media.

Computational Communication Methods

I develop methods for studying communication at scale. I combine topic modeling and network analysis with close reading to examine how online discussions form and evolve. My work on Content Engagement Capacity uses networks to measure how different ways of framing an issue help generate conversation.
