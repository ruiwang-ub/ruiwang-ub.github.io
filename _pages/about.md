Before joining UB, I gained two years of teaching experience at Anhui Normal University in the School of Journalism and Communication. Utilizing novel computational approaches and quantitative methods, my research explores the primary drivers and discourses surrounding the politicization and polarization of non-political topics such as science, health, and technology in online spaces. Additionally, I am engaged in multiple projects investigating the discussions and impacts of AI on public opinion, technological acceptance, and news trust. My work has been recognized with prestigious awards, including the Best Student Paper Award and a travel grant from APSA. My research has been published in top-tier journals such as New Media & Society, Communication Research, and Information, Communication & Society. In my leisure time, I am an Er-Hu player and an avid pop music fan, and I have maintained a consistent weekly running routine for over a year.
