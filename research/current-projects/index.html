Current Projects

  • Auditing AI search. I compare 16 AI search models to examine whether question wording and a user’s disclosed political identity are associated with which news outlets the systems cite.
  • How readers judge AI answers. I’m designing an experiment to test how readers evaluate an AI-generated answer when its position and the sources it cites align with or challenge their own views.
  • AI disclosure and human oversight. I study how explaining the roles of AI and human reviewers affects audience judgments of news and fact-checks.
  • Political identity and disagreement on Reddit. I analyze Reddit discussions about abortion to examine how people express political identities and disagreements across online communities.

If you’re curious about any of these projects, I’d be happy to tell you more. I’d also love to hear what questions you’re exploring and talk about ways we might work together.
